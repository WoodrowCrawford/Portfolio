A Deep Dive

A small game I made for a game jam where the theme was current.
You play as a sailor who has to collect all the bottles while avoiding the enemies!

Project Overview

A game jam I worked on by myself where the theme was "current." Made in a short time frame, it's focused on the theme and one tight gameplay loop. You play as a sailor collecting all the bottles in the water while dodging enemies. It's built to be difficult and a little tedious on purpose, but rewarding once you finally clear it.

Key Features

  • Top down 2D gameplay
  • Collect bottles while enemies chase you and hazards block your path
  • Each bottle you grab spawns another somewhere new

Development Details

  • Role: Sole Developer
  • Engine: Unity
  • Language: C#
  • Status: Completed
  • Platforms: Windows, Web Browsers

What I worked on

A quick solo jam game for the theme "current." I wanted a simple, tight loop: collect the bottles, don't die. What I built:

  • A collect loop where grabbing a bottle spawns the next one at a random spot, so you're always chasing the next one
  • Enemies that home in on the player, plus hazards that punish you for cutting corners
  • The usual backbone, spawners, a game manager, HUD, and audio

Development Insights

This was a game I made by myself for fun. I wanted something challenging and a little tedious, but satisfying to finally beat. It was good practice sharpening my Unity and C# skills, and another rep at taking a game all the way from idea to finished.
