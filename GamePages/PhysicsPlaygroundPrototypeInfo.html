Project Overview

A physics prototype I built in Unity for a college project. You can click on objects to launch them into the air, fire projectiles that use real physics to hit a target, and there's a small level with a key, a locked door, and hazards to get through. I made it to dig into Unity's physics engine and the math behind it.

Key Features

  • Click any object to launch it with a burst of force
  • A projectile launcher that solves the physics to hit a target
  • A key and locked door mechanic and hazards to navigate
  • Real physics-driven interactions throughout

Development Details

  • Engine: Unity
  • Language: C#
  • Status: Completed
  • Platforms: Windows

What I worked on

A college prototype for getting hands-on with Unity's physics, where the projectile math ended up being the fun part. What I built:

  • Click to launch, raycasting to whatever object you click and hitting it with an impulse force
  • A projectile launcher that works out the physics equations to figure out the exact velocity needed to reach a target in a set time
  • A little bit of a game around it, a key, a locked door, hazards, and an enemy

Development Insights

I built this for college to really understand Unity's physics engine, not just turn it on. The part I got the most out of was the projectile launcher, where I had to work through the actual kinematics equations to calculate the launch velocity to hit a target. It sharpened both my C# and my grasp of the math that physics in games is built on.
