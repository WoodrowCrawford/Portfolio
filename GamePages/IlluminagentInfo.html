Illuminagent
A Deep Dive

A game jam game I helped develop in Unity for a game jam.
You play as a person who is tasked with bringing light to a dark world filled with enemies.

Project Overview

In the cosmos, a realm of darkness threatens to swallow the universe. You play as a radiant source of light, sent from world to world to illuminate dark planets and push back the looming darkness.

Key Features

  • Unique light-based gameplay mechanics
  • Choose your own difficulty
  • Challenging, replayable runs

Development Details

  • Role: Programmer (team project)
  • Engine: Unity
  • Language: C#
  • Status: Completed
  • Platforms: Web Browsers

What I worked on

This was a team game jam game, and my job was the enemy AI, getting the enemies to move and react as the player brings light into the dark world. The game ended up ranking #51 in the jam, which was a great feeling for something we put together in a jam's time frame.

Development Insights

This was a collaborative game jam project where I handled the enemy AI. Working with a team on a tight jam deadline, and landing at #51, taught me a lot about collaborating under pressure and how much clear communication matters when everyone's moving fast.
