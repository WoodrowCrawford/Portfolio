Project Overview

Spooky Mansion 3 is a horror game set in a haunted mansion full of nasty surprises. The goal is to make your way through the mansion, solve puzzles, and uncover the dark secrets hidden in its walls, if you can escape.

Key Features

  • A creepy, horror atmosphere
  • Randomized events and encounters
  • Weapon mechanics
  • Stealth elements

Development Details

  • Role: Programmer (team project)
  • Engine: Unity
  • Language: C#
  • Status: Demo (made for a game jam)
  • Platforms: Web Browsers

What I worked on

This was a team game jam game, and my job was the enemy AI, the part that makes the enemies hunt and react to the player as they sneak through the mansion. Like a lot of jam games it didn't get fully finished in time, but the core stealth-and-AI loop came together.

Development Insights

This was a game jam project where I handled the enemy AI. We ran out of time before everything was in, which is just how jams go sometimes, but it was good practice building stealth-style AI on a deadline. I learned a lot about scoping realistically and getting AI working quickly when the clock's against you.
