Laureli: Blood-Dipped Manor
A Deep Dive

A funded vampire murder-mystery visual novel I'm helping a team build, with a demo out now and a Steam release on the way.

Project Overview

Laureli: Blood-Dipped Manor is a vampire murder-mystery visual novel I'm working on with a team, built in Ren'Py. You play as a detective staying at her uncle's manor when a maid turns up dead, a storm traps everyone inside, and a vampire is hiding among them. It's a character-driven mystery with branching investigation, dialogue choices, and multiple endings. The demo is out now on itch, as well as steam.

Key Features

  • A character-driven vampire murder mystery
  • Branching investigation and dialogue choices with multiple endings
  • Around 25,000 words of story, with full voice acting
  • Rated 4.8/5 on itch, with the demo entered in four different game jams

Development Details

  • Role: Programmer (team project)
  • Engine: Ren'Py
  • Language: Python
  • Status:Demo available on Steam and itch.io, with a full release coming soon
  • Platforms: Windows, macOS, Android

What I worked on

This is a real production with a full team of writers, artists, voice actors, and programmers, and it's raised over $2,000 in funding so far. I'm one of the programmers on it, handling a lot of the day-to-day work that keeps development moving:

  • Adding character sprites into scenes and getting them positioned and behaving right
  • Implementing audio throughout the game
  • Tracking down and fixing bugs and other issues as they come up

Development Insights

This was the first fully funded project I've been a part of, raising over $2,000. Not only have streamers played it live, but it was also my first game I helped develop thats also on Steam. Working on it has been a different kind of learning: instead of building my own systems from scratch, I got to work with a codebase other people built, figuring out how everything fits together, and make changes without breaking what's already there. It's taught me a lot about what it's like to contribute to a real production instead of running my own.
