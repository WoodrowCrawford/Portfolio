Cold Case
A Deep Dive

A fatal accident, an abandoned house, a poltergeist – as straightforward as cases go. Nothing to worry about, right?

Project Overview

Cold Case is a branching horror visual novel I worked on with a team for the Spooktober VN Jam. You play a paranormal investigator on your first day, sent to an abandoned house where a fatal accident happened and something is still lingering. It is a character driven mystery with a branching story and two endings, around 18,000 words, built in Unity using the Naninovel engine.

Key Features

  • A character driven horror mystery set in an abandoned house
  • A branching story that leads to two different endings
  • Around 18,000 words, about an hour and a half per playthrough
  • Built in Unity with the Naninovel visual novel engine Hand drawn artwork and an original soundtrack

Development Details

  • Language: C#
  • Engine: Unity
  • Status: Completed
  • Platforms: Windows, Web

Development Insights

Cold Case was built in Unity with Naninovel rather than Ren’Py, so the scripting worked differently from a lot of my other visual novel work. Most of the job was turning a finished script into a playable build under a jam deadline: wiring up the scenes, getting the branching paths to land cleanly on both endings, and keeping the sprites and audio in step with the writing. Naninovel has its own way of handling commands and flow, so a lot of it was learning that and fitting my work around what the writers and artists had already made.
